Effective date: 19 August 2026
Provided by [DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME] ("we"), developer of the EndScroll iOS app.

EndScroll helps you spend less time scrolling. It is built to work locally on your device: most of what you do in the app never leaves your iPhone. This policy explains what does leave, why, and how to delete it.

1. What we collect

2. What stays on your device

The following is stored locally and is not uploaded to us:

  • Screen Time data. App blocking uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. Apple's design keeps your app and usage data on the device — we never receive the list of apps you block or how long you use them, and we could not transmit it even if we wanted to.
  • Your roadmap progress, habits, sleep records, focus sessions and challenge history.
  • Diagnostic logs. These stay on the device unless you explicitly export and send them to us for support.
  • Notification schedules and content.

3. Who processes data for us

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — stores your account and the preferences listed above.
  • RevenueCat — validates your App Store receipt and tells the app whether your subscription is active. It receives your account identifier and purchase data. We do not send it your name or email.
  • Apple — handles Sign in with Apple, all payments, subscription billing and refunds. We never see your payment details.

These companies act as our service providers and are only permitted to process data to provide their service to us.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads and do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the app does not ask for tracking permission.
  • We do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

5. Payments and subscriptions

EndScroll Plus is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple charges you, not us. Payment, renewal, price changes, cancellation and refunds are handled entirely by Apple, and you can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your Apple Account subscription settings. We receive only your subscription status, never your card or payment details.

6. Deleting your data

If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account from inside the app: Profile → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your cloud document and your authentication account, revokes the Sign in with Apple authorisation, and wipes local app data. It cannot be undone.

Two things to know:

  • Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Apple bills subscriptions against your Apple ID, and no app can cancel one on your behalf. Cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Your subscription record at RevenueCat is retained for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. To have it erased as well, email us at the address below.

You can also email [CONTACT EMAIL] to request deletion, a copy of your data, or correction of it. We will respond within 30 days.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your account and preferences for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the cloud document and authentication record are deleted immediately. Backups may retain copies for a short period before being overwritten. Purchase records are retained as required for tax and accounting obligations.

8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Data at rest with our providers is encrypted using their standard infrastructure protections. Access is limited to the people who need it to operate the app. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

EndScroll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, email us and we will delete it.
